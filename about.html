About the Hub

A collaborative initiative for growth and innovation

Growth and Innovation Hub brings together practical education, leadership development, and community-focused collaboration to help organizations and individuals grow.

Who we are

Helping organizations, leaders, and communities grow

Growth and Innovation Hub is a collaborative initiative focused on organizational development, practical AI education, leadership development, preparedness, entrepreneurship, and community impact.

The initiative serves organizations and individuals through training, workshops, educational programs, and collaborative development opportunities, with businesses, nonprofits, churches and ministries, schools, community organizations, leaders, and entrepreneurs.

What guides us

The principles behind the work

Every focus area, workshop, and partnership is shaped by the same commitments.

  • Practical education

    Learning people can apply right away in their organizations, teams, and businesses.

  • Collaboration

    Growth happens together, across organizations, sectors, and communities.

  • Innovation

    Helping organizations understand and thoughtfully adopt new tools and ideas, including AI.

  • Community advancement

    Strengthening the leaders and organizations that serve their communities.
